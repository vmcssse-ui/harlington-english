01

Three things, every week

Every week on this page asks for the same three things, in the same order. If you do these three, you are keeping up.

1

Listen

Open the read-along and press play. The words light up as they are read. Listen to the whole story once before the lesson.

Headphones in, eyes on the words.
2

Read

Open the lesson page. Read the big question and the key words. Then read the story along with the voice, out loud if you can.

Arrive already knowing a little.
3

Practise

Cover the key words and write them from memory with their meanings. Say the week's line aloud the way the storyteller would.

Recognising a word is not knowing it.
